### B. Test #24 — Inside vs. Outside Surface Conservation (`SURFACE_PREREGISTRATION.md`)
Two geometric traps will invert or blur this test if coded literally as pre-registered:

1. **Global Median Radius Measures *Caps vs. Belly*, Not *Lumen vs. Outside*:**
   `SURFACE_PREREGISTRATION.md` (lines 25–29) splits exposed residues at the global median radial distance `r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2)` from the symmetry axis. Because a vault tapers from `r ~ 200 A` at the waist down to `r ~ 55–70 A` at the cap ring while the shell wall is only `~15–20 A` thick:
   - **Every *outward*-facing residue on the shoulder, cap-helix, and cap-ring** (`r ~ 60–145 A`) falls *inside* the global median radius and gets mislabeled **"lumen-facing"**;
   - **Every *lumen*-facing residue on the inner barrel wall of R1–R7** (`r ~ 180–190 A`) falls *outside* the global median radius and gets mislabeled **"outward-facing"**.
   - **Fix (commit before running):** Classify each exposed residue against the **local shell midsurface radius `R(|z|)` at its own axial height `z`** (`Delta_r_i = r_i - R(|z_i|)`, plus axial normal at the flat cap top). Also separate **subunit-subunit lateral/waist interface residues** (buried in the 78-mer, exposed in the isolated monomer) as their own category — that is likely where structural packing selection lives.

2. **`data/raw/struct/4V60.cif.gz` Asymmetric Unit Is Only One Half-Vault (39 Chains, Not 78):**
   Checking coordinate headers only: `4V60.cif.gz` has `39` chains (`31,668` C-alpha atoms; `z` in `[-58.9, +327.8] A`). That passes the `>= 30 chains` gate as-is (`SURFACE_PREREGISTRATION.md` line 51), leaving the **entire N-terminal R1 waist dimerization interface open to solvent** instead of buried. Generating the full 78-mer biological assembly via `_pdbx_struct_oper_list` before computing SASA fixes it.

1. **Why `LEDGER #22` ("Dispensable but Conserved": `pLI = 0`, `LOEUF = 0.92`, yet `mis_z = 2.50` & `84–90th` pct identity) Isn't a Paradox:**
