# Review of the vault papers

Reviewed the committed texts of `papers/PAPER1_vault_reassessment.md` and `papers/PAPER2_bench_experiments.md` at `d12e880` (2026-11-07 08:08, "Ledger count 63 -> 68"). That commit already absorbs a second-agent pass (ledger entries 61–65) and corrects the header count. Nothing in the papers, the ledger, the results, or the analysis code was modified for this review.

Numbers below were checked against `results/*.json`, `results/surface_residues.csv`, `argon/results/test_003_pore_and_electrostatics.json`, `argon/results/audit_005_papers.json`, and the local text of Hornebeck 2026 and Osinski 2026. Analysis scripts were not re-run.

## What holds

The revised text is careful in places that were previously wrong, and many of the headline counts match the files.

Phosphorylation. `phospho_part2.json` has 64 primary rows, two of them `MVP_acetylprotein`. Of the other 62, none have q < 0.05. The strongest is UCEC S445 versus PARP4, rho −0.374, p 0.00103, q 0.066. The six S445–PARP4 rows have sample-weighted mean rho −0.0358 on n = 612, with three negative and three positive signs. The secondary count is 12 against 18.93 expected, binomial p 0.018.

Surface conservation. `surface.json` has lumen-facing exposed mean 0.722, outward-facing 0.640, difference −0.081, CI −0.139 to −0.026, Spearman −0.206. Recomputing the Spearman p from the 352 exposed rows in `surface_residues.csv` gives 9.6×10⁻⁵.

Shoulder geometry. All 78 ligand copies sit at fractional depth 0.34 (`pocket_geometry.json`). Ligand exterior solvent accessibility is 0.00 Å² at probe radii 1.4 Å and 2.5 Å, and the maximum exterior channel radius to the ligand is 1.10 Å (`test_003_pore_and_electrostatics.json`). One adjacent figure is not in the paper: the same file gives the apo 30-residue pocket 20.93 Å² of exterior surface at 1.4 Å, on 6 atoms. The ligand is sealed from the outside. The pocket mouth is not.

Domain conservation, as revised. On the human-indexed alignment in `audit_005_papers.json`, the shoulder (499–665) has median identity 0.829 and mean 0.780, against protein-wide median 0.782 and mean 0.730. Repeat 8 median is 0.888. The PARP4-contact span 160–260 has mean 0.711. Restricted to residues present in `surface_residues.csv`, shoulder median is 0.859 and the mapped protein median is 0.796. F547, D566, F567 and G569 are 1.000 in that table; K506 is 0.987. Calling the shoulder the most conserved structural domain, and saying single repeats can beat it, matches these files.

Other counts that match. Lung malignant cells: 21 patients, 24,784 cells, acute–chronic axis at the 29th percentile (28.8), interferon signatures at the 95th and 88th (`sc_kim.json`). gnomAD: LOEUF 0.92, observed/expected LoF 0.74, mis_z 2.50, 1 homozygote against 0.62 expected. Unadjusted co-abundance: PARP4 r = 0.820 and TEP1 r = 0.520, ranks 1 and 2 after dropping MVP itself, across 13 cohorts and 10,924 proteins. CCLE replication is 375 lines, and there too PARP4 and TEP1 are the top two partners. LRP: 7 of 18 unique drugs at q < 0.05; methotrexate is rank 4; mitoxantrone is the weakest of the three vtRNA binders (rho +0.064). Matched mediation medians are 25.0% (ABCB1) and 10.6% (division rate) on the seven-drug set in `lrp_mediate_matched.json`. CYP3A4 partial rho is −0.052, p 0.33, n = 366, and CYP2E1 is the only nominal hit. Vaultrc5 liver log2FC is −1.37 and the 17-gene autophagy mean is −0.058, p 0.46. Dictyostelium log2FC +3.49 and +1.61 are in `crossspecies.json`. Shoulder formal charge +23.4 e apo to −132.6 e holo is in the pore JSON; the paper's +23 and −133 are that pair rounded.

Hornebeck 2026, local text, lines 706–738: isolated human and mouse TEP1 TeTIR dropped HEK293 NAD+ to about 30–40% of control at 24 h, and wild-type TEP1 produced DRAQ7-positive death from about 20 h, absent in E1008A. The sentence "No cyclic ADPR products were detected in reactions with TEP1 TeTIR under these conditions" is in that file; a newline and indentation sit between "were" and "detected". Osinski 2026, lines 321–328 and 370–412: only TEP1 among the proteins they tested hydrolysed NAD+ and NADP+, activity required E1008, apparent Km was about 6.9 mM for NADP+ and not determined for NAD+, purified reconstituted MVP+TEP1 particles hydrolysed added NAD(P)+, and added BAD density sat in the shoulder pocket. The paper's retreat from "NAD+ entry requires opening" follows those paragraphs.

## Errors still in the revised Paper 1

**Copy-number adjustment did not leave the ranks unchanged.** Section 3 says PARP4 and TEP1 remain the two closest proteins after per-gene copy-number adjustment. `cna_adjusted.json` says otherwise. Unadjusted ranks are PARP4 1 and TEP1 2. Adjusted ranks are PARP4 1, ACTR3 2, TEP1 3. ANXA2 moves from 12 to 7. That run is also a different experiment: 10 cohorts and 10,077 proteins, because three cohorts had no usable copy-number match. Three-principal-component removal and the 375 cell lines do keep TEP1 at rank 2. The sentence joins those to the copy-number result, and only the copy-number clause is false. TEP1 at r = 0.520 is also only 0.008 above ACTR3 (0.512) before any adjustment, so "the two closest" was already a thin lead.

**The development result is labelled uninformative in its own output, and the paper reports it as a finding.** `development.json` has `gate_pass: false` and `label: "UNINFORMATIVE"`. The pre-registration required LIN28A, LIN28B and IGF2BP1 each at or below the 10th percentile in at least 5 of 7 organs, for at least 2 of the 3. IGF2BP1 clears 6 organs. LIN28A is absent from the filtered matrix in five organs and is not below the 10th in the two where it is measured (ovary 58th, testis 56th). LIN28B is at or below the 10th in three organs (brain is 11.8). The introduction says a failed gate is reported as uninformative rather than reinterpreted. Section 2 then says MVP rises at the 94th–95th percentile in six of seven organs and uses that rise to retire the scaffold hypothesis. The percentiles are real: brain 94.8, cerebellum 95.2, heart 94.2, kidney 94.8, liver 95.0, ovary 94.5, testis 82.5. The decision rule says they are not to be interpreted. The same omission, smaller, applies to the persister test: `persister_primary.log` prints `UNINFORMATIVE` because a proliferation control was missing, while the paper quotes 80.6, 82.0 and 81.1 against the 90th-percentile bar and does not say the gate failed. Those three numbers are below the bar either way.

**"Held under purifying selection" misses the pre-registered bar.** `divergence.json` stores percentile 89.5 and `label: "TYPICAL"`. The pre-registration defined unusually conserved as at or above the 90th percentile of species- and length-matched groups, and typical as between the 10th and the 90th. The vertebrate-restricted null, which is the fairer one because 70% of the MVP sequences are vertebrate, is the 84.2nd percentile (`divergence_clade.json`, 84.19 on 253 groups). Missense z = 2.5 with LOEUF 0.92 is mild missense depletion and no loss-of-function constraint. The abstract's "sequence-constrained" and "held under purifying selection" describe a result the registered rule called typical. The 89.5 and 84.2 figures themselves are reported accurately in section 3.

**The threefold turnover is a summary, not the result in each cell type.** The abstract says PARP4 turns over threefold faster (22.2 h versus 66.4 h) in four of four cell types. The four measured pairs in the ledger, taken from Mathieson, are B cells 16.0 versus 55.9 (3.5×), hepatocytes 19.4 versus 29.4 (1.5×), monocytes 26.0 versus 77.0 (3.0×), and NK cells 38.2 versus 310.3 (8.1×). The ordering PARP4 < TEP1 < MVP holds in all four. The pair 22.2 and 66.4 is the proteome-table summary in `turnover.json` (percentiles 9.8 and 58.6). No cell type has both of those half-lives. `turnover.json` also records verdict `UNINFORMATIVE` because the short-lived controls are absent from the pulse-SILAC table. The written rule allowed the test to proceed; the paper does not mention the conflict between the script's verdict and the rule.

**The vtRNA median still uses the duplicated drug row.** Section 3 says binder and non-binder median rho are +0.098 and +0.099. On the 19 rows in `lrp.json`, which list Docetaxel twice, the non-binder median is 0.0986. After dropping the duplicate, it is 0.0893. The vtRNA median stays 0.0977 because the middle of the three binders is doxorubicin. The correction to "7 of 18" was applied. The correction to the median that the duplicate had inflated was not. Mitoxantrone's q of 0.43 is the Benjamini–Hochberg value on the 19-row list.

**"No co-essentiality with any gene" is false on the same page as the number that refutes it.** Section 3 gives the strongest genome-wide correlation as r = 0.205, and `coessentiality.json` has 2,886 genes at q < 0.05 out of 17,916, with PAPPA at the top. Section 3b then lists "no co-essentiality with any gene" as part of the pattern the enzymology explains. The partners are not coupled: PARP4, TEP1 and ANXA2 rank 9,020, 11,734 and 14,985. The genome-wide statement is a different claim, and the file's own verdict on this test is `UNINFORMATIVE` because CAV1 did not recover CAV1's own complex. Section 3 says that. Section 3b uses the null anyway.

**Section 4's scorecard contradicts section 2.** The scorecard says single-cell transcript coupling succeeded, and then says every measurement of steady-state coupling to an external pathway failed. The 95th and 88th interferon percentiles are steady-state coupling to an external pathway. So is the developmental rise, if it is admitted at all.

**"On identical complete-case samples" overstates the mediation file.** `lrp_mediate_matched.json` has `n_complete` 132, but the per-drug sample sizes are 131, 132, 131, 132, 132, 120 and 131. Etoposide is 120. Within a drug the two covariates may share a sample. Across drugs they do not.

**The class split is not clean.** Docetaxel is a tubulin agent. In the matched file, division rate absorbs 67.5% of its MVP coefficient and ABCB1 absorbs 35.1%. Paclitaxel, vinorelbine and vinblastine go the other way. The two absorptions are from separate models, so they are not shares of one effect and they can sum past 100%. "Split cleanly by class: efflux substrates to ABCB1, S-phase agents to division rate" does not survive docetaxel.

**Different half-lives do not require PARP4 to leave the shell.** Section 3 says a 22-hour PARP4 half-life against a 66-hour MVP half-life requires reversible dissociation or active extraction. Pulse-SILAC measures the protein population. PARP4 is substoichiometric on the particle, so a free pool is expected, and the free pool can dominate the measured half-life while the docked molecules stay for the life of the shell. The paper already says the measurement is not an off-rate. The next sentence treats it as one.

**p = 0.058 is not a falsification.** The IP-MS overlap is 12 against 7.4 expected, hypergeometric p = 0.058. The amoeba overlap is 32 against 25.0, p = 0.067, and the authors' own orthogroup correction pushes that p above 0.20. Both are failures to clear a pre-registered 0.05 line. The point estimates are enrichments. "Falsified it twice" and "physical association narrows to PARP4, TEP1 and, in humans, ANXA2 and S100A10" are stronger than the tests. CAPN2, EHD4 and IQGAP1 are among the co-abundance neighbours and are absent from the IP table, so they were not tested. ANXA2 and S100A10 have nominal IP p-values near 0.002. That is not a multiple-testing-corrected call on 1,205 proteins.

**Section 6b still undercounts the corrections.** The header now says `LEDGER.md` has 68 entries, and the table has 68 rows (highest id 65, plus 13a, 26b and 34b). That count matches. Section 6b still says two analyses were materially wrong before review: the mediation subcohorts and the *Dictyostelium* name-matching. The ledger's own later rows add the phospho miscount (#59), the mitoxantrone superlative (#60), the NAD-opening inference (#61), the copy-number conflation (#63), and a collaborator's summary statistic copied without opening the JSON (#64).

**A sensitivity that changes the interferon label is omitted.** The project's own results page says depth adjustment moves the lung acute–chronic placement from the 29th percentile to the 9.7th, which is across the 10th-percentile line the page uses for a specific call. The paper reports the 29th.

## The enzymology section

The retreat from "the cage keeps TEP1 away from NAD+, and that is why the cell is alive" is the right retreat. Hornebeck measured the lethality of the isolated domain. Osinski measured a millimolar unactivated Km. Mvp-null animals are viable. Purified vaults hydrolyse NAD+ added to the tube. SARM1 is a cytosolic autoinhibited NADase with no vault around it. Those four points break the old keystone, and the revised section 3b states them.

What replaced it still explains more of the scorecard than the enzymology can reach. Autoinhibition of TEP1 accounts for viable knockouts, no growth phenotype, and no bulk NAD+ depletion. It does not account for the co-loss null, the phosphorylation null, the CYP3A4 null, the tissue ranking, the LRP decomposition, or which face of the shell is conserved. Section 3b lists several of those as "the pattern we observed" and says the enzymology explains them. They were collected first. The text does say the ordering was post hoc. The verb "explains" still covers results the enzyme does not speak to.

Two stoichiometry sentences are not in the files they depend on. "Roughly 8–20 copies" of PARP4 per 78-mer: Osinski reports an average of about 20 PARP4 molecules per reconstituted vault, which is an overexpression assembly. The 8 is not in that paragraph. "Roughly one to two copies" of TEP1 per particle, and "some fortyfold scarcer than MVP": neither figure is in the local Osinski, Hornebeck, or Lodwick texts, and neither is in a results file. Osinski could not resolve TEP1 in the single-particle maps. The fortyfold figure is 78/2. It should be cited to the paper that measured it, or dropped.

The route given for dinucleotide entry is an inference stacked on two different observations. Osinski shows that purified particles hydrolyse added NAD(P)+ and that BAD density appears in the shoulder after nucleotides were added during the reconstitution experiment. That shows access under those conditions. It does not identify the door. Collins 2026 showed pre-formed vaults taking up AAV capsids, which the same section classifies as macromolecular traffic that requires opening. Using that paper as evidence that a 663-dalton metabolite enters unaided mixes the two size classes the correction was written to separate.

The CCLE NAD sentence is directionally fair and slightly selective. Pearson r = +0.077 for MVP versus NAD+ is the figure in the argon metabolomics output, and it is not a depletion. The same screen's top hit was 1-methylnicotinamide, which collapsed after NNMT adjustment (r from +0.41 to +0.04). The paper cites the flat NAD correlation and does not mention the hit that had to be thrown out. A reader of section 3b cannot tell that the metabolomics were not uniformly null before the confounder check.

## Paper 2

Constraint 1, the product panel, the phospho count, and the experiment ordering notes were brought into line with Paper 1. Three problems remain.

Experiment 3 still opens with the claim the constraint above it retracts. "Why now" says cytosolic NAD+ cannot reach the pocket in the closed particle. The design paragraph twelve lines later says labelled-NAD+ uptake is the wrong assay because free NAD+ already reaches intact cages. Those cannot both be the reason to run the experiment.

"MVP-null mice die of Pseudomonas aeruginosa" compresses Kowalski et al., Science 2007;317:130. After an experimental intranasal inoculum, MVP-null mice had about 3.5-fold more bacteria in the lung and a hazard ratio for death of 2.96 (n = 15 null versus 17 wild type, 96 hours). The paper's mechanism was failed epithelial internalization after CFTR-dependent raft recruitment. The mice do not die of Pseudomonas in ordinary housing. The sentence then says Pseudomonas and listeriolysin O are both pore-forming toxins. Pseudomonas is an organism. InlK, the Listeria protein that binds MVP, is not listeriolysin O; the published interaction is surface coating that avoids autophagy. A pore-forming-toxin trigger can still be tested. Kowalski 2007 and the InlK paper are weak priors for it, and Paper 2 presents them as the convergence that motivates the top-ranked experiment.

The cADPR warning is now accurate. The primary sentence is in the Hornebeck text.

## Bottom of the report

The arithmetic that was corrected in this revision — phospho, the shoulder superlative, the NAD-must-open inference, the methotrexate rank, the Hornebeck lethality, the millimolar Km — checks out against the files and the two primary texts read here.

The claims that do not survive are concentrated. TEP1 is not still rank 2 after copy-number adjustment. The development rise is a failed-gate result reported as a result. Conservation at the 89.5th percentile was pre-registered as typical, and the vertebrate-matched figure is the 84.2nd. The threefold half-life gap is not present in every cell type named. The vtRNA non-binder median is still the duplicated-table number. "No co-essentiality with any gene" contradicts the co-essentiality file. The half-life gap does not establish that docked PARP4 leaves the shell. The TEP1 copy number used to retire the old keystone is not in the cited texts. Paper 2's best experiment is still motivated by a misstatement of Kowalski 2007 and by a sentence that contradicts its own constraint.
