MVP did not partition with the chronic interferon program

Partition analysis on public data, run 2026-11-06. Predictions were committed to git before any expression value was read.

The prediction failed. It said MVP would track the chronic set (ISG.RS) more than the acute set (IFNG.GS) inside malignant cells. No dataset, model, or compartment came out chronic.

What the data shows instead: MVP rises with interferon state in malignant cells, more than most genes do, but does not choose between the two programs. It also shows no anti-correlation, which was the containment prediction.

How to read it. Δ below zero leans acute, above zero leans chronic. A label counts as specific only at the 90th genome percentile or higher for chronic, or the 10th or lower for acute, since an average gene has a negative Δ here too.

What this does to the hypothesis

  • Signaling-brake layer: the committed abandonment condition is met for "MVP is a member of the chronic program." This test can only speak to that claim.
  • Containment layer: its only testable prediction here, anti-correlation with both sets, failed. Containment as a physical function is untouched, because this test cannot reach it.
  • Published contradictions: the hoped-for resolution did not appear. Thyroid, pancreas, and liver all sit on the same side of the axis (section 5).

Watch for a third absorption. Transcript co-variation cannot see a protein-level action, so MVP's reported STAT1 inhibition is neither supported nor refuted. That is true, and it is also the sentence a dying hypothesis would say. If the chronic-program idea continues, it needs a new prediction that can fail, ideally MVP knockout with an interferon-output readout.

4. Could this test have found a chronic gene?

Yes. Δ separates held-out ISG.RS members from IFNG.GS members with AUC 0.78 to 0.79 in malignant cells. MVP's Δ falls at the 11th percentile of ISG.RS members in lung and the 30th in melanoma, well below most genes the test identifies as chronic.

The one real positive: in lung malignant cells MVP's coefficient is at the 95th genome percentile for ISG.RS and 88th for IFNG.GS. In melanoma, 97th and 78th. Absolute effects are small.

6. Corrections to the plan, and limits

  • MVP is in Benci's IFNG.GS, contrary to plan §6. That set is exactly Hallmark IFN-γ minus ISG.RS, so MVP landed there by construction and nobody tested it.
  • The two planned single-cell datasets are controlled access (dbGaP phs002371, EGA EGAS00001005021). I used GSE131907 for lung and GSE115978 for melanoma. Lung malignant labels are mostly stage IV, and it is not an ICB cohort.
  • IFNG.GS came from patent claim text (US12662705), since Benci's Table S1 was blocked. ISG.RS matched Qiu's source data at 38 of 38.
  • Correlative throughout. MVP detected in about 54% of lung malignant cells. Melanoma has 14 usable patients; melanoma macrophages were unusable.
  • I drafted the pre-registered predictions from the plan, and they are not your independent statement. You can add a dated dissent, which will not change what was frozen.
